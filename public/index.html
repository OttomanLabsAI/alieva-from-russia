The method

Forget traditional textbooks

You learn Russian the same way you learned your first language as a child — through context. Your brain absorbs grammar and vocabulary organically, without you even noticing.

📖

Stories, videos and natural speech — tailored to your level

Instead of memorising rules out of context, you consume interesting stories, watch engaging videos and listen to real Russian. It’s natural, it’s effective — and most importantly, it’s fun.

FAQ

Good questions

Do I need to know the Cyrillic alphabet before starting? +
Not at all. Complete beginners are welcome — we cover the alphabet naturally in the early lessons through the same story-based approach, so it never feels like a hurdle.
How does the monthly subscription work? +
The Exclusive Russian Club is 8€ per month with fresh materials every week. You can try it free for 3 days and cancel anytime — no lock-in.
What if I’m terrible at grammar? +
Perfect — you’re exactly who this method is for. We don’t drill tables. You absorb grammar through stories and context, and when we do explain it, we focus on the logic, not memorisation.
How much time do I need to spend studying every week? +
Less than you think. Even 15–20 minutes of comprehensible input a day moves you forward. The flagship course runs at 3 video lessons a week, and everything is recorded, so you set the pace.
